# Wild Pollinator Notes

## Overview

Wild pollinators include solitary bees, hoverflies and bumblebees, and they do not live in managed hives. Their contribution is easy to overlook because it is not rented, counted or moved between orchards.

## Solitary Bees

Solitary bees nest in bare ground or in hollow stems rather than in colonies. They forage at lower temperatures than honeybees and often fly in light rain, so they can pollinate a bloom week that is too cold for a hive.

## Habitat

Hedgerows, unsprayed field margins and bare ground are the habitat that sustains them. Removing the margin to gain a few rows of crop usually costs more pollination than the extra rows return.
